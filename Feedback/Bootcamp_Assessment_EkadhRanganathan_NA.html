B. Conceptual Understanding of Autocorrelation

Evidence of understanding: - Constructs adjacent-year vectors (year_i, year_j) with correct (n-1) pairing. - Uses correlation to quantify dependence and uses permutation to generate a null where temporal order is broken.

Gaps: - No explicit discussion of lag choice beyond 1-step; that’s fine if only lag-1 is required. - One-tailed p-value uses permuted_cors > actual_cor; ties and direction could be clarified.
